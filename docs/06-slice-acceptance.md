# First 60-second playable slice — acceptance
- Hero: original blue/white police mecha **placeholder**, not official franchise model. Camera behind character; robot running and car form.
- Input: keyboard W/A/S/D, Space, E; optional touch input.
- World: compact but visibly layered Tech Forest, gate, energy station, energy cells, final portal.
- Interactive tutorial: near the reactor, the three-color wiring puzzle starts without E/F. Player selects wires and matching sockets; three matches open the gate visibly.
- Sound: original browser-generated melody and effects, user gesture required to start; mute control always available.
- Feedback: story objective stays visible, correct answers animate environment, no fail-state punishment.
- QA: unit tests, TypeScript, production build, real-browser smoke test, then a real play session with a 5-year-old. No performance guarantees until measured on actual devices.
- **Non-claims:** not a full 10-level game; no official Blue Cop model, license, branded audio, or completed cinema-quality rig.
